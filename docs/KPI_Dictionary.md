# KPI Dictionary

| KPI | Definition | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| EV Registrations | Number of newly registered EVs in the synthetic market | Indicates demand scale |
| EV Share | EV Registrations / Total Car Registrations | Shows adoption maturity |
| Public Charging Points | Synthetic number of public charging points | Indicates infrastructure build-out |
| Charging Density | Charging Points / EV Registrations × 1,000 | Compares infrastructure relative to EV base |
| Addressable Market | Synthetic B2B component opportunity in EUR million | Market-sizing input |
| Market CAGR | Annualized growth between 2021 and 2026 | Identifies growth markets |
| Commercial Ease | 1-5 judgement score for go-to-market practicality | Adds execution reality |
| Competition Intensity | 1-5 judgement score for competitive pressure | Avoids ranking only by market size |
| Country Attractiveness | Weighted score combining size, growth, adoption, infrastructure, ease and competition | Prioritization tool |
| Segment Attractiveness | Weighted customer-segment score | Helps select target customers |
| Accessible Share | Portion of the total market considered realistically reachable | Revenue scenario input |
| Capture Rate | Assumed share of the accessible opportunity | Revenue scenario input |
