# Business Questions

I structured the project around a few questions that could come up in a Market Intelligence, Business Development, Strategy or Business Analyst role.

## Market attractiveness

1. Which European EV markets are largest in 2026?
2. Which markets grew fastest between 2021 and 2026?
3. Which countries already have high EV adoption?
4. Which countries still appear to have adoption headroom?
5. How mature is the public charging infrastructure?

## Country prioritization

6. How should market size, growth, adoption, infrastructure, commercial ease and competition be balanced?
7. Which countries should be treated as immediate focus markets?
8. Which markets are interesting but better suited to a later phase?

## Competition

9. Which competitors have the broadest product portfolios?
10. Which competitors compete through technology differentiation?
11. Where is pricing pressure likely to be strongest?
12. Is there a positioning gap between premium technology and lower-cost regional players?

## Customer strategy

13. Which customer segments offer the best combination of opportunity, strategic fit and sales practicality?
14. Should the fictional company focus first on OEMs, Tier-1 suppliers, charging operators or other channels?

## Revenue scenarios

15. What could a conservative, base and upside commercial case look like?
16. How sensitive is the result to accessible market share and target capture rate?

The point of the project is not to produce one 'correct' strategy. It is to build a structured way to compare different market-entry choices.
